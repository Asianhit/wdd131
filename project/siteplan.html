HavenGrid Real Estate

Site Purpose

The purpose of HavenGrid Realty is to provide an intuitive, responsive, and interactive digital platform where users can seamlessly explore, filter, and inquire about real estate properties while learning about the agency’s services.

Typography

To ensure brand consistency and strong visual hierarchy, HavenGrid Realty utilizes two Google Fonts:

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif)
    Applied to: Header titles, <h1>, <h2>, and <h3> tags. Chosen for its structural and modern architectural aesthetic.
  • Body Font: Open Sans (Sans-serif)
    Applied to: Main body text, paragraphs, list items, and form inputs. Chosen for high readability and clarity across all screen sizes.

Target Audience Scenarios

Scenario 1: First-Time Homebuyer

"I am looking for a modern 2-bedroom home in a specific price range. Can I quickly filter properties by my budget on one page and save my favorites?"

Solution: The Properties page features a dynamic JS filter bar and a 'Save to Favorites' feature using localStorage.

Scenario 2: Property Seller

"I want to sell my property. What services does HavenGrid Realty offer, and how do I contact an agent?"

Solution: The Services page outlines listing options, and the Contact page provides a direct inquiry form.
